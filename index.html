Our mission

Taking Africa's mining sector to the next level: connecting its producers with global buyers, and raising the bar to international standards.

Africa holds some of the world's most important mineral resources, yet many independent producers still sell without independent testing, clear contracts or access to global markets. We want to change that, starting with the specialist minerals that larger traders overlook, one well-run transaction at a time.

The next level

Helping independent mines present their material professionally, with verified records, proper testing and complete documentation.

Global buyers

Connecting African producers with smelters and industrial buyers in Asia, South America and beyond.

Raising the bar

Preparing every transaction to recognised international practice, so that African supply is judged on the same terms as any other.

The international standards our process follows

  • OECD Due Diligence GuidanceThe reference for responsible mineral supply chains: knowing where material comes from and how it was produced.
  • ISO/IEC 17025 laboratoriesTesting by laboratories accredited to the international standard for testing competence.
  • Independent inspectionSampling, weighing and quality checks at loading by an inspector neither side controls.
  • Incoterms® 2020Delivery terms defined in the language global buyers already use, so risk and cost are clear.
  • KYC and anti-briberyKnowing every counterparty, and paying local partners only for documented work.

Saxum Metals follows these frameworks in how it prepares transactions; it does not hold them as certifications. Accreditation is held by the laboratories and inspectors appointed for each deal.

    Why specialist minerals

    Where specialist knowledge matters

    Tin, barite, bentonite and vermiculite are small markets compared with iron ore or copper, but the industries that use them cannot run without them.

    Overlooked by large traders

    Smaller volumes and specialised buyers mean good African material often never reaches the right market. We focus where others don't.

    Specification is everything

    A barite cargo lives or dies on its specific gravity, and vermiculite on its grade and asbestos testing. We test before we offer.

    Essential, steady demand

    Drilling, steelmaking, electronics, construction and farming use these minerals every day, in every market.

    How it works

    Four steps, agreed in writing

    1. We agree the requirement

      Product, grade limits, quantity, delivery point and payment terms.

    2. We check the producer

      Company, licences and authority to sell the material on offer.

    3. Independent testing

      Inspectors and laboratories sample and test the material.

    4. We prepare the deal

      Offer, contracts, permits and logistics, with the parties and their advisers.

    We act as an agent. We are paid a fee agreed in writing. We don't own mines, hold stock or handle cargo payments: the buyer pays the seller directly.
